JadOO DSP is a system-wide audio processor for Android. It shapes the sound of the music app you already use, in real time, entirely on your phone.

No accounts. No ads. No internet access. Nothing leaves your device.

EQUALIZERS
• 15-band graphic EQ from 25 Hz to 16 kHz at ISO centre frequencies
• 16-band parametric EQ: peak, shelf, pass, band-pass, notch and all-pass filters, with a draggable response graph, Q control and preamp
• Save, recall and back up your own presets

BASS
• Analog Bass: vintage-console-style bass with Drive, Warmth and Drift, plus a passive-EQ style boost/cut
• JadOO DBFB: level-aware sub-bass weight
• Mobile Bass: fuller low end on your phone's own speaker

CLARITY AND SPACE
• Hi-Res Upscaler: recovers treble detail lost to compression
• HDR Dynamics: restores punch to loudness-war masters
• Harmonic Exciter: presence and sparkle in the 2–8 kHz range
• Tube Warmth: valve-amp style bloom and softened highs
• Surround+: wider stereo image while vocals stay centred
• Crossfeed (beta): softens the hard left/right split of headphones

SMART LISTENING
• Loudness Contour keeps tonal balance at low volume
• Per-app profiles switch automatically when an app starts playing
• Separate settings for each headphone, speaker and Bluetooth device
• SBC Enhancement conditions audio for Bluetooth headphones that use the SBC codec
• Precise gain staging to keep stacked boosts from clipping

WORKS WITH YOUR PLAYER
JadOO DSP attaches to music apps that support Android's audio-effect sessions, and appears in their "Equalizer" or "Audio effects" menu. Some players and some phone manufacturers restrict this; if a player isn't detected, check its equalizer settings.

A notification stays visible while the audio engine runs, so Android doesn't stop it mid-song.
